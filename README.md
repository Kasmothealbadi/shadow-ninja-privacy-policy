Shadow Ninja: Dodge & Survive · Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how kasmo the al badi we handles information when you play the mobile game Shadow Ninja: Dodge & Survive ("the Game"). By using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect
We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location directly.

Stored on your device only: your high score and game settings (music volume, SFX volume, control layout). This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.
Collected automatically by third-party services (see section 2): advertising ID, device model, operating system version, IP address (approximate location), language, and ad interaction and crash/performance data.

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded ads ("Watch Ad to Continue"). To do this we use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob [and/or Unity Ads – edit to match what you use]. These services may collect and use the data listed above to show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. They have their own privacy policies:

3. How we use information
To run the Game and save your progress and settings on your device.
To display ads, which help keep the Game available.
To improve stability and gameplay.
4. Your choices
You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads) or iOS settings (Privacy → Tracking).
You can delete all locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the Game.
5. Children's privacy
The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

6. Data sharing and security
We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the ad and analytics providers described above. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure.
